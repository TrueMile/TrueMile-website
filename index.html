Our approach

Own the trucks.
Connect the decisions.

A load’s rate is only part of the story. Empty miles, driver hours, fuel stops, and the next available load determine what a truck can earn.

We’re building agents to consider those decisions together, inside a fleet we operate ourselves.

Inside our operation

Four agents.
One operation.

Finds freight across spot markets and existing customers. Brings suitable opportunities into view for our fleet.

Brings freight, capacity, and customer commitments into a coordinated weekly plan. Gives the operation a shared starting point.

Our story

Built from the dispatch floor.

I started running my family’s trucking business at 19 and have generated more than $7M in trucking revenue.

I started building tools for our own dispatch operation because I lived the same problems every day: finding freight, keeping trucks covered, adjusting plans, and keeping everyone informed.

Those tools became TrueMile. Now we’re building the trucking company around them.

Harpreet Dhaliwal · Founder
